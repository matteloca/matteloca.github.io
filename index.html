Who I am

My interest in data started early and became a discipline in Bologna, where I earned a bachelor's and master's degree in Statistics. A conversation with a professor from Milan on the potential of Big Data set the direction of my career — from statistical analysis to leading product analytics for sensor-rich, IoT-scale systems. That path eventually brought me to Boston, where I've built my career, and, more recently, my citizenship: I'm a U.S. citizen now.

What I Do

I'm a statistician and product analytics leader focused on turning high-frequency sensor and behavioral data into decisions that improve products and customer outcomes. At Cambridge Mobile Telematics, I lead analytics for connected-device and telematics initiatives operating at multi-million-device scale — product experimentation, adoption forecasting, cohort and survival analysis, KPI design, data-quality monitoring, and customer-facing insights. I work across product, engineering, data science, and customer teams to move from raw telemetry to clear decisions, with a particular interest in sensor-rich products: telematics, IoT, wearables, energy, and smart-home systems.
